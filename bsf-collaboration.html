BSF Collaboration

The Binational Science Foundation (BSF) collaboration supports joint research between Israeli and American scientists, fostering scientific exchange and advancing fundamental research in theoretical high energy and nuclear physics.

About the BSF

The BSF was established in 1972 to promote scientific relations between the United States and Israel. It funds collaborative research projects in a wide range of scientific disciplines, enabling researchers from both countries to work together on fundamental questions in science.

This collaboration advances our understanding of Quantum ChromoDynamics (QCD), heavy-ion collisions, and related phenomena at the frontier of theoretical physics.
