About the Collaboration

This collaboration is supported by the United States–Israel Binational Science Foundation (BSF), an organization established in 1972 to promote scientific relations between the two countries by funding joint research projects.

The collaborative work spans fundamental questions in theoretical high energy and nuclear physics, including the behavior of strongly-coupled matter at extreme conditions, QCD dynamics at high energies, and the properties of Quark-Gluon Plasma as studied at the LHC and RHIC.
